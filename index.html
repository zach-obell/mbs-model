Methodology

How the model works

  1. Refinancing incentive = WAC (rate homeowners pay) − current mortgage rate, month by month along the rate path.
  2. Prepayment speed from an S-curve: CPR = base turnover + max refi ÷ (1 + e−k × (incentive − midpoint)), converted to a monthly rate SMM = 1 − (1 − CPR)1/12.
  3. Cash flows for each remaining month: scheduled payment at the WAC, interest to the investor at the 5.5% coupon, scheduled principal, and prepayment = SMM × remaining balance.
  4. Discounting at Treasury 5/10-yr blend + MBS spread, moving with the shock and path. The sum of discounted cash flows is the price per $100.
  5. Duration and convexity by repricing at ±50bp and ±100bp static shocks.

Assumptions and limitations

  • One pool of identical average loans; real pools mix rates and ages.
  • The MBS spread is the latest public figure and is held constant until a newer one is published.
  • One rate scenario at a time: no Monte Carlo rate paths or option-adjusted spread (OAS).
  • No burnout, seasoning ramp, or lock-in drag: deep out-of-the-money pools (e.g. 2.0s) actually prepay below the model's turnover floor.
  • Mortgage rates and discount yields move one-for-one; payment delay and defaults are ignored (the agency guarantee makes defaults look like prepayments).
